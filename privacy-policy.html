Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Yallago mobile application, operated by worldsoft, collects, uses, stores, protects and, where necessary, shares users' personal data. This page may be published as a public web page to meet store compliance and partner requirements.

1. Who we are

Yallago is a travel booking mobile application operated by worldsoft. The application enables users to search, book and purchase airline tickets, accommodation and related travel services.

2. Data we collect

We may collect identification data, contact data, travel data, booking data, payment-related data processed through secure providers, technical data and marketing preference data.

3. Purposes of processing

Your data may be used to create and manage your account, search and book travel services, send confirmations and e-tickets, process payments, prevent fraud, provide support, improve services, comply with legal obligations and send commercial communications where consent is required.

4. Legal basis for processing

Depending on applicable law, we process data based on contract performance, legal obligations, legitimate interest and consent where required.

5. Data sharing

Your data may be shared, strictly where necessary, with airlines, hotels, GDS systems, APIs, aggregators, payment providers, technical service providers and competent authorities. We do not sell or rent personal data.

6. Data retention

We retain data for as long as necessary for account management, booking fulfillment and applicable legal retention periods. After that, data is deleted, anonymized or securely archived.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal data. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent and, where applicable, data portability.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

If public web pages or related web services use cookies or similar technologies, they may be used for technical operation, security, analytics, user experience improvement and, where applicable, marketing.

10. Minors

Yallago is not intended for independent use by minors without authorization from a parent or legal guardian.

11. International transfers

For certain international bookings, some data may be processed or transferred outside the user's country where necessary to perform the requested service.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any updated version will be published on this page and/or within the application.
